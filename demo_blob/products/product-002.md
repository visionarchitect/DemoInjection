# Meridian AirSense 8

Manufacturer: Meridian Controls
Category: Environmental sensing

AirSense 8 monitors temperature, humidity, and particulate conditions in production areas. Its wall-mount enclosure supports quick installation by facilities teams.

- Multi-sensor environmental readings
- Local status display
- PoE and low-voltage power options

Operating range: -10 to 55 C
Connectivity: Ethernet, MQTT
Enclosure: IP54
