# Meridian Controls PlantCam EC-2
Manufacturer: Meridian Controls
Category: Industrial camera

PlantCam EC-2 is a fixed industrial camera for visual checks at conveyor and packaging stations.

Features:
- 5 MP monochrome sensor
- Adjustable machine-light trigger
- Compact aluminum housing

Resolution: 2592 x 1944
Interface: GigE Vision compatible
