# Contoso Dynamics PowerMeter PM-80

Manufacturer: Contoso Dynamics
Category: Smart power meter

The PM-80 provides panel-level energy measurements for factories and distribution rooms. A clear front display gives operators quick access to current load information.

Features: three-phase monitoring; configurable alarms; Modbus export
Specs: Voltage: 480 VAC; Accuracy: 0.5%; Display: 3.5 inch
