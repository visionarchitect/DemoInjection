# NovaEdge IG-30 Industrial Gateway
Manufacturer: NovaEdge
Category: Industrial gateway

The IG-30 bridges legacy serial equipment with modern plant networks for small production cells.

Features:
- Dual Ethernet interfaces
- Modbus TCP and RTU translation
- DIN rail mounting

Specs: Serial ports=2; Ethernet=2 x 1 GbE; Temperature=-20 to 60 C
