BluePeak Instruments VibraSense V2
Manufacturer: BluePeak Instruments
Category: Vibration sensing

VibraSense V2 is a battery-powered vibration sensor for rotating equipment. It helps maintenance teams compare machine health readings over time.

Features: tri-axis accelerometer; magnetic mount; scheduled sampling
Technical specifications: Battery life=24 months typical; Radio=LoRa demo band; Frequency=10-1000 Hz
